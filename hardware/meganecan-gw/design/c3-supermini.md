# ESP32-C3 SuperMini — socket footprint spec

Daughterboard socketed onto the MeganeCAN GW carrier via **2 × 1×8 2.54 mm female headers**.
This document is the authoritative geometry + pin-order source for the
`meganecan.pretty/MODULE_ESP32-C3-SUPERMINI.kicad_mod` footprint.

## 1. Verified board facts (web research)

| Property | Value | Source confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Board outline | **22.52 × 18.0 mm** (some sources round to 22.5 × 18) | HIGH — 3 independent sources agree |
| Pin count | 16, as two 1×8 rows along the two **long** (22.52 mm) edges | HIGH |
| Pin pitch (within a row) | **2.54 mm** (0.1″) | HIGH |
| Row-to-row spacing | **15.24 mm** (0.6″) | HIGH — ProtoSupplies states 15.24 mm explicitly; consistent with 18 mm width (1.38 mm pad-centre to board edge each side) |
| Row pin span | 7 × 2.54 = **17.78 mm** (fits inside 22.52 mm length) | HIGH — derived |
| USB connector | USB-C at one short (18 mm) end, overhangs the outline slightly | HIGH |

## 2. Pin order (TOP VIEW, USB-C at TOP / −Y)

```
                 ┌───── USB-C ─────┐
             ┌───┴─────────────────┴───┐
   1   5V  ○─┤ .                     . ├─○  16   GPIO5
   2   GND ○─┤                         ├─○  15   GPIO6
   3   3V3 ○─┤                         ├─○  14   GPIO7
   4  GPIO0 ○─┤     ESP32-C3          ├─○  13   GPIO8   (blue LED, inverted)
   5  GPIO1 ○─┤     SuperMini         ├─○  12   GPIO9   (BOOT button)
   6  GPIO2 ○─┤                         ├─○  11   GPIO10
   7  GPIO3 ○─┤                         ├─○  10   GPIO20  (U0RXD)
   8  GPIO4 ○─┤                         ├─○   9   GPIO21  (U0TXD)
             └─────────────────────────┘
```

- **Left row, top→bottom (nearest USB first):** 5V, GND, 3V3, GPIO0, GPIO1, GPIO2, GPIO3, GPIO4
- **Right row, top→bottom (nearest USB first):** GPIO5, GPIO6, GPIO7, GPIO8, GPIO9, GPIO10, GPIO20, GPIO21
- **Corner nearest USB-C:** pin 1 = **5V** (left) and pin 16 = **GPIO5** (right).
- **Corner farthest from USB-C:** pin 8 = **GPIO4** (left) and pin 9 = **GPIO21** (right).

Numbering is standard DIP/counter-clockwise: pin 1 top-left → pin 8 bottom-left → pin 9
bottom-right → pin 16 top-right.

> The task brief listed the left row as `5V, GND, 3V3, GPIO4, GPIO3, GPIO2, GPIO1, GPIO0`
> (descending). Research **contradicts** this: the GPIO run is **ascending away from USB**
> (GPIO0 adjacent to 3V3, GPIO4 at the far corner). Two independent transcriptions of the
> vendor pinout diagram (espboards.dev, mischianti.org) agree on ascending. The corrected
> ascending order is used above and in the JSON.

### Mounting-orientation note (no mirroring required)

The C3 plugs component-side-up into female headers on the GW carrier's **top** layer. Viewed
from above, the C3's left row lands on the carrier's left column. The footprint below is drawn
in top view, so pad X signs match the diagram directly — do **not** mirror.

## 3. Nets used by MeganeCAN GW

| Pad | Pin | Net | Function |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 5V | `+5V` | rail from Mini-360 buck (also the USB backfeed maintenance path) |
| 2 | GND | `GND` | ground |
| 8 | GPIO4 | `MMCAN_TX` | multimedia CAN TXD → SN65HVD230 #2 pin D |
| 7 | GPIO3 | `MMCAN_RX` | multimedia CAN RXD ← SN65HVD230 #2 pin R |
| 9 | GPIO21 | `LINK_C3_TX` | → WROVER GPIO19 (link UART2 RX) |
| 10 | GPIO20 | `LINK_C3_RX` | ← WROVER GPIO18 (link UART2 TX) |

Pads 3 (3V3), 4–6, 11–16 are **not connected** on the carrier but must still be placed as
mechanical/electrical THT pads — leave them as no-connect pads with silk labels so the socket is
fully supported and future expansion is possible. Do **not** tie pad 3 (3V3) to the carrier's
3.3 V rail (generated by `U5` LM2596S-3.3 — the AMS1117 this note originally referred to was
deleted by power.md §7): the C3 has its own onboard LDO and back-driving it risks contention.

> **Cross-domain note.** The C3 runs off +5V and keeps running when the 3V3 rail is dead
> (USB-only bench mode). Its outputs `GPIO21` (link TX) and `GPIO4` (MMCAN TX) face silicon on
> the 3V3 rail, so each of `LINK_GW_RX`, `MCAN_TX` and `MCAN_RX` carries a **1 k series
> resistor** (`R13`/`R14`/`R15`) at the C3 end. Without it the C3 back-powers the dead 3V3 rail
> through those pins' ESD clamp diodes. See DESIGN.md §7.3.

## 4. Footprint geometry

Origin = geometric centre of the 16-pad array (also the board-outline centre in X and in the pin
span; the USB end overhangs).

- **Pad type:** THT, round, plated
- **Drill:** **1.00 mm** (generous for hand assembly; typical female-header pin is 0.64 mm sq)
- **Pad diameter:** **1.80 mm** (0.40 mm annular ring)
- **Pad 1 shape:** rectangular/square (1.80 × 1.80 mm) to mark pin 1; pads 2–16 round
- **Column X:** −7.62 mm (left, pads 1–8) / +7.62 mm (right, pads 9–16)
- **Row Y:** −8.89 mm (nearest USB) … +8.89 mm in 2.54 mm steps
- **USB-C direction:** −Y
- **Board outline (F.Fab):** rectangle X ∈ [−9.00, +9.00], Y ∈ [−11.26, +11.26] (18.0 × 22.52 mm)
- **Courtyard (F.CrtYd):** X ∈ [−9.25, +9.25], Y ∈ [−12.50, +11.51] — extra 1.0 mm at −Y for the
  USB-C connector shell overhang and mating cable strain relief
- **Silk:** outline rectangle offset 0.15 mm outside F.Fab, broken at pads; a **bold "USB ▲" bar
  and the word `USB-C` printed at the −Y end**, outside the courtyard, so the assembler cannot
  install the module rotated 180°. Also silk-label pin 1 with a filled triangle plus text `1 5V`.
- **Reference `U?` / value:** place on F.SilkS **outside** the −Y end or beside the +X row; the
  module body hides anything inside the outline.

### Keep-out under the module

Socketed on 2.54 mm female headers the C3 underside sits ≈ **8.5 mm** above the carrier. The
inter-row corridor is 15.24 − 1.80 = **13.44 mm** of free width. Parts placed under the module
must be ≤ 7 mm tall and should be reflow/hand-solderable *before* the headers are fitted —
prefer to keep this area for GND pour and routing only.

## 6. Before fab — physical verification checklist

1. **Measure the actual module** with calipers: row-to-row centre distance and the 17.78 mm span.
   15.24 mm is well attested but SuperMini clones vary.
2. **Photograph the silkscreen** and confirm the left row reads `5V G 3V3 0 1 2 3 4` downward with
   USB up — this is the one item where published sources disagreed.
3. Confirm GPIO20/GPIO21 order at the far corner (see uncertainty note below).
4. Dry-fit the module into the female headers before soldering the headers to the carrier.
